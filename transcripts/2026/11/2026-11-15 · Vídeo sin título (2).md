How to Build
AI Agent with Claude
in 10 Minutes
No coding, No complex setup. Just Claude + a few simple steps.

STEP 1
Go to Claude (claude.ai)
Sign in to your account (or create one if you're new)

STEP 2
Use this promt
"I want to build an AI agent. Ask me the right questions one by
one to set it up. I'm new to this, so keep it simple and beginner-
friendly."

STEP 3
Add a skill (optional but powerful)
Go to Skills section and enable useful skills like: Web Search,
File Reader, or Code Interpreter. This helps your agent to do
more.

STEP 4
Connect your tools
Go to the Connectors section and add the tools you need.
Examples: Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Slack, or custom APIs.
Just click, authorize and connect.

STEP 5
Set up your agent
Give your agent a name, description and define its goal.
Example: "Help me to manage my emails and summarize
important messages today."

STEP 6
Configure settings
Adjust the behavior, response style and permissions. You can
choosw how it should respond, what tools to use, and what it
can access.

STEP 7
Test your agent
Try a few real tasks. Ask it something like: "Search for the latest
AI news and give me a 3-point summary".

STEP 8
Make it live
If everything works, save the agent. Now you can use it anytime
in Claude or through the web app.

STEP 9
Improve over time
See what works, tweak the prompt, add more skills or tools, and
make it even better, The more you use it, the smarter it gets.

STEP 10
Enjoy you AI agent!
You just built your first AI agent with Claude in 10 minutes. Now
go build something amazing!
