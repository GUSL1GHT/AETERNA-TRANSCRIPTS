# Cómo crear un agente de IA con Claude en 10 minutos

15/11/2026 · TikTok · [Ver el vídeo](https://vm.tiktok.com/ZN85XG7K5/)

## De qué va

Carrusel en inglés con 10 pasos para montar un agente de IA en claude.ai sin programar: un prompt para que Claude te guíe con preguntas, activar skills, conectar herramientas, definir el objetivo, probarlo y mejorarlo con el uso.

## Ideas

- Empezar con el prompt que hace que Claude te pregunte paso a paso cómo montar el agente.
- Darle al agente un objetivo concreto (p. ej. gestionar y resumir el correo) y probarlo con tareas reales antes de usarlo a diario.

## Lo que dice el vídeo

El vídeo no tiene narración: es un carrusel de texto en inglés. Texto de las diapositivas:

**How to Build AI Agent with Claude in 10 Minutes.** No coding, no complex setup. Just Claude + a few simple steps.

**Step 1.** Go to Claude (claude.ai). Sign in to your account (or create one if you're new).

**Step 2.** Use this prompt: "I want to build an AI agent. Ask me the right questions one by one to set it up. I'm new to this, so keep it simple and beginner-friendly."

**Step 3.** Add a skill (optional but powerful). Go to Skills section and enable useful skills like: Web Search, File Reader, or Code Interpreter. This helps your agent to do more.

**Step 4.** Connect your tools. Go to the Connectors section and add the tools you need. Examples: Gmail, Google Drive, Notion, Slack, or custom APIs. Just click, authorize and connect.

**Step 5.** Set up your agent. Give your agent a name, description and define its goal. Example: "Help me to manage my emails and summarize important messages today."

**Step 6.** Configure settings. Adjust the behavior, response style and permissions. You can choose how it should respond, what tools to use, and what it can access.

**Step 7.** Test your agent. Try a few real tasks. Ask it something like: "Search for the latest AI news and give me a 3-point summary".

**Step 8.** Make it live. If everything works, save the agent. Now you can use it anytime in Claude or through the web app.

**Step 9.** Improve over time. See what works, tweak the prompt, add more skills or tools, and make it even better. The more you use it, the smarter it gets.

**Step 10.** Enjoy your AI agent! You just built your first AI agent with Claude in 10 minutes. Now go build something amazing!
